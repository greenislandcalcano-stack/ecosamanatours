Explore the Wild Side of Samaná

Get ready for an exciting ride through the tropical countryside of El Limón. You will drive your own buggy along rural trails, pass through small communities and enjoy several refreshing stops surrounded by beaches, palm trees and Dominican nature.

The experience combines off-road adventure, local culture, beautiful beaches and a visit to one of the area's most refreshing rivers.

Places You Will Visit

1

Playa Las Canas

Enjoy time at this peaceful tropical beach, take photos and cool off in the ocean.

2

Traditional Dominican House

Discover local countryside traditions and taste Dominican coffee, handmade chocolate, cacao and mamajuana.

3

Playa Morón

Visit one of the most beautiful beaches in the El Limón area and enjoy another refreshing swim.

4

Río El Limón

Finish with a refreshing visit to Poza de Doña Blanca, a natural swimming area in Río El Limón.

What the Excursion Includes

  • Guided buggy route through tropical countryside trails.
  • You drive your own buggy.
  • Visit and swimming time at Playa Las Canas.
  • Visit to a traditional Dominican countryside house.
  • Tasting of coffee, cacao, handmade chocolate and mamajuana.
  • Visit and swimming time at Playa Morón.
  • Visit and refreshing swim at Río El Limón.
  • Local guide and basic safety orientation.

Key Information

  • Availability: every day
  • Driving: guests drive their own buggy

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothes that may get dusty or muddy
  • Bathing suit and towel
  • Closed shoes or secure sandals
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Bandana or scarf for dust
  • Waterproof phone protection
  • Cash for souvenirs, lunch and tips
